our mission

Data-driven democracy starts in Kenya

We believe every candidate deserves access to real intelligence — not just well-funded incumbents. Kampeni levels the field with AI-powered analytics that are Swahili-native and built for Kenyan political reality.

Why we're building this

Kenya's 2022 election saw 14,000+ candidates competing for seats across 47 counties. The sophisticated ones had Nairobi consultants monitoring WhatsApp groups and compiling spreadsheets manually.

The rest — the local MP challenger, the first-time MCA candidate, the woman running for governor in a rural county — had nothing. No data. No intelligence. Just instinct.

Kampeni ingests every major Kenyan news source in real time, classifies sentiment in English and Swahili, tracks opponent mentions, and synthesizes it into a morning briefing any candidate can act on.

Our principles

Kenya-firstBuilt for Swahili and Sheng from day one. Not English models retrofitted for East Africa.
Privacy by defaultCandidate data is never shared. Each campaign sees only their own intelligence.
Fair accessPricing designed so a first-time MCA candidate can afford the same tools as a gubernatorial campaign.
Accuracy over speedWe'd rather deliver one verified insight than ten noisy ones.

platform features

Everything your campaign needs

Built module by module, shipped live. No bloated software — every feature earns its place.

✓ Live Now

News Intelligence

Every 30 minutes, Kampeni scrapes Nation Africa, Standard Media, and Citizen Digital. New articles are deduplicated, stored, and queued for analysis automatically.

  • 3 major Kenyan outlets
  • RSS + HTML scraping
  • 30-minute cadence
  • Duplicate detection
✓ Live Now

Sentiment Analysis

Every article is classified positive, negative, or neutral using VADER for English and a Swahili keyword model. Month 2 upgrades to a fine-tuned Llama 3.1 8B.

  • English + Swahili support
  • Score: −1.0 to +1.0
  • Language auto-detection
  • Sheng upgrade Month 2
✓ Live Now

Opponent Tracker

Register any opponent with name, aliases, and constituency. Every 5 minutes the system scans articles for mentions and extracts the exact sentence as context.

  • Name + alias matching
  • Context extraction
  • Title vs body detection
  • Mention timeline
✓ Live Now

Field Agent Network

Field agents submit structured reports from any ward — support level, top issue, GPS. Reports persist to the database within 5 minutes via Celery.

  • 7 issue categories
  • 5 support levels
  • GPS coordinates
  • Ward aggregation
Month 2

Social Media

Facebook page monitoring via Meta Graph API and X/Twitter ingestion. Public posts, comments, and engagement from your constituency.

  • Meta Graph API
  • X/Twitter Basic
  • Comment sentiment
  • Engagement tracking
Month 4

AI Daily Briefing

At 11 PM, GPT-4o synthesises the day's news, opponent activity, and field reports into a brief. Delivered to your phone via SMS at 6 AM.

  • GPT-4o generation

  • 6 AM daily delivery
  • Editable before send
